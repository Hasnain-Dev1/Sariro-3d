# Sariro — Product Vision, UX & UI Master Reference

> **Read this before designing or building any feature.**
> This file exists so Sariro never drifts into being "another Udemy / Codingal /
> Unacademy". Every product decision should be checkable against this document.

---

## 1. What Sariro is

Sariro is a global learning platform built on one belief:

> **Every human learns differently. Education should adapt to the learner, not
> force every learner into the same curriculum.**

Sariro is **not**:
- primarily a coding platform
- an exam-preparation platform
- another Udemy / Coursera / Codingal / Unacademy
- a course marketplace or content library

**Long-term vision:** a *global secondary school for learning itself* — where a
child, teenager, university student, professional or lifelong learner can come to
discover, understand, practice and master something they want to learn.

The goal is not to teach information. It is to teach people:

> **How to learn. How to think. How to solve problems. How to become the
> commander of their own mind.**

---

## 2. Core philosophy

| Traditional education asks | Sariro asks |
|---|---|
| "What should every student learn?" | "Who is this learner, what do they want to become, what do they already know, and what should they learn next?" |

**Traditional focus:** syllabus · grades · exams · attendance · completing chapters · completing courses

**Sariro focus:** curiosity · understanding · mastery · experimentation · problem solving · projects · independent thinking · continuous growth

The product must therefore *feel* fundamentally different from a conventional LMS.

---

## 3. The core promise

> You don't come to Sariro to complete a course.
> You come to Sariro to **become capable of something.**

A learner should be able to say:
- "I want to understand physics."
- "I want to become good at mathematics."
- "I want to build my first application."
- "I want to understand artificial intelligence."
- "I want to understand how businesses work."
- "I want to finally understand something I never understood in school."

Sariro turns that intention into a personalised learning journey.

---

## 4. Sariro is for everyone

Not a kids' product. Learners include children, teenagers, university students,
professionals, entrepreneurs, career changers, hobby learners, parents — anyone
curious.

**Age should not define the product. Curiosity should.**
A 10-year-old learning astrophysics and a 35-year-old learning programming should
both feel they belong.

---

## 5. The learner is the centre

Do **not** make the homepage a catalog of courses. Do not open with
"Python Course / Java Course / Physics Course."

Ask instead:

> **What do you want to learn?** — or — **What do you want to become?**

The system should gradually come to understand the learner.

- *"I want to become an aerospace engineer"* → maths, physics, programming,
  engineering concepts, communication, problem solving, projects, research.
- *"I want to build my own company"* → business, economics, communication,
  technology, marketing, psychology, leadership, financial literacy, projects.

**The journeys must not be identical.**

---

## 6. The Sariro learning loop

```
DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → APPLY → CREATE → REFLECT → MASTER → DISCOVER SOMETHING NEW
```

Learning should feel **active**. Sariro is not a place where users watch videos.
Video is one tool among many. Encourage: questions, exercises, experiments,
projects, discussions, challenges, reflection, creation, real-world application.

---

## 7. Mastery, not completion

Never make *course completion* the ultimate achievement.

❌ `73% Course Completed`

✅
```
Your Mastery
  Mathematical Reasoning — 72%
  Problem Solving        — 81%
  Python Fundamentals    — 64%
  Independent Learning   — Growing
```

An achievement should represent **demonstrated ability**, not consumed content.
The learner should be able to say: *"This is what I can actually do."*

---

## 8. The Sariro AI

AI is deeply integrated but is **not the product's identity** — it is the
intelligence layer underneath.

Over time the AI should understand: who the learner is, what they know and don't
know, their interests, pace, struggles, motivation, goals, and prior mastery.

It should behave like a **highly intelligent mentor, not a generic chatbot**:

> "You seem comfortable with algebra. Let's challenge you with something harder."
> "Before we continue, a concept from fractions is causing difficulty here. Let's fix that first."
> "You said you want to understand astrophysics. You're ready for orbital mechanics."

---

Not a grid of course thumbnails. Personal, calm, intelligent.

---

## 10. Do NOT make it look like an LMS

**Avoid:** excessive course cards · giant banners · aggressive promo sections ·
"BUY NOW" · discount badges · fake urgency · % - off marketing · endless course
grids · cluttered dashboards · excessive gamification · childish cartoon UI ·
generic AI-chatbot layouts.

**Sariro should feel:** intelligent · calm · premium · human · futuristic ·
trustworthy · educational · global · sophisticated.

It should feel like a place where **serious learning** happens.

---

## 11. Visual identity

Communicate: **Intelligence + Curiosity + Possibility.**

Premium technology feel — *without* becoming another generic "AI startup" site.

Use: generous whitespace · excellent typography · subtle motion · clean cards ·
soft depth · elegant transitions · strong hierarchy · restrained colour ·
beautiful visualisations where useful.

Avoid excessive gradients and flashy effects. It should still look excellent in
five years.

---

## 12. Landing page

Do **not** open with "Learn Python Online." Open with the philosophy.

**Hero:** *Learn anything. Become anything.*

**Support:** *Sariro is a new kind of learning school built around you — your
curiosity, your pace, your goals, and the person you want to become.*

**Primary CTA:** Start Learning · **Secondary:** Discover Sariro

---

## 13. Name the problem

Traditional education assumes: **One curriculum. One pace. One definition of success.**

But humans are different.

> **Different learner. Different journey. Different outcome.**

This should be one of the strongest ideas on the site.

---

## 14. Parent experience

Parents are critical. Don't show *"Your child completed 8 lessons."*

Show meaningful growth:
```
Your child's growth
  Problem Solving         ↑ 14%
  Communication           ↑  9%
  Mathematical Reasoning  ↑ 18%
  Independent Learning    ↑ 21%
```
Plus qualitative observation:
> "Your child has started attempting problems independently before asking for help."

The parent should feel: **"Sariro understands my child."** That emotional trust
matters enormously.

---

## 15. Teacher / mentor experience

Mentors are guides, facilitators, challengers — not lecture-deliverers.

Give mentors real insight into the learner:
> "This learner understands the concept but struggles to apply it independently."

So humans can focus on what actually matters.

---

## 16. Learner profile

A living representation of the learner:
- **About me** — interests, goals, curiosities
- **What I know** — concept mastery, skills, knowledge areas
- **What I'm building** — projects, experiments, research
- **My growth** — problem solving, creativity, communication, critical thinking, independent learning
- **My journey** — past, current, future

---

## 17. Projects are first-class

Learners **create things**: websites, applications, experiments, research,
presentations, businesses, games, scientific investigations, writing, art,
engineering projects.

The portfolio becomes one of the most valuable parts of a learner's identity.

> ❌ "I completed a Python course."
> ✅ "I built this."

---

## 18. Global by design

Architecture should allow for multiple languages, currencies, education systems,
age groups, cultures, international mentors, global communities, localised
journeys.

But **do not clutter the current UI with i18n controls.** Global infrastructure
underneath; the experience stays simple.

---

## 19. Navigation

Keep it simple and learner-intent shaped (names can change):

`Home · Learn · Build · Explore · Progress · Community · Profile`

> Don't make users navigate the organisation's structure.

---

## 20. Philosophy to reinforce repeatedly

- Every learner is different.
- Every learner can grow.
- Curiosity is valuable.
- Failure is part of learning.
- Understanding > memorisation.
- Mastery > completion.
- The learner owns their journey.
- The ultimate goal is **independent learning**.

---

## 21. The emotional experience

| Moment | Should feel | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Opening Sariro | "This place understands me." | "Here are 4,000 courses." |
| Parent opens it | "My child is becoming more capable." | "My child watched another lesson." |
| Finishing something | "I can do this now." | "I received a certificate." |
| Getting stuck | "Sariro will help me figure this out." | "I'm bad at this." |

---

## 22. The ultimate vision

- *"I don't know what I want to learn."* → Sariro helps them discover.
- *"I know what I want to become but not how."* → Sariro creates a path.
- *"I tried this before and failed."* → Sariro finds another approach.
- *"I already know this."* → Sariro doesn't force repetition.
- *"I'm fascinated by something outside my field."* → Sariro helps them explore.
- *"I'm 45 and want to start physics."* → **"Let's begin."**

---

## 23. The product rule

> **Does this make the learner more capable, more curious, more independent, or
> more capable of learning?**

If no — reconsider the feature.

- Don't build features because competitors have them.
- Don't build features to increase screen time.
- Don't optimise course completion at the expense of actual learning.
- **Optimise for capability.**

---

## 24. Long-term ambition

Something between a school · a mentor · a university · a library · a laboratory ·
a community · a personal tutor · a career-exploration system · an AI learning
companion — and ultimately **its own category**.

Not to replace teachers. Not to replace schools overnight. To **redefine what
education looks like** when technology, AI, great teachers, personalisation,
projects and human curiosity are designed around the learner.

---

## Final design principles

| Choose between | Choose |
|---|---|
| More content **or** better learning experience | **Better learning experience** |
| More features **or** more clarity | **Clarity** |
| Learner dependent on Sariro **or** capable of learning independently | **Independence** |

> Sariro should not try to own the learner.
> Sariro should empower the learner to own their own mind.

---

## Core statement

> **Sariro is a global learning school built around one idea: every human learns
> differently. We don't teach people what to become. We help them learn how to
> become whatever they choose.**
